*Digest - 2026-08-20*

_TL;DR: The largest crypto exchange opened live trading to AI agents, and Stripe confirmed a $7.5B buy of the default model router._

1. *Binance Agent OS lets ChatGPT, Claude Code, and Cursor place live trades*
   Binance launched Agent OS, connecting MCP plus ChatGPT/Codex, Claude Code, and Cursor to market data, accounts, and order execution. Agents run in dedicated subaccounts with withdrawals blocked by default; Binance does not cap losses beyond what you fund, and it cannot see the agent's reasoning.
   Why it matters: the decision this week is whether to fund a sandboxed subaccount for agent trading or keep agents read-only.
   https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/20/binance-now-lets-ai-agents-trade-but-keeping-them-in-check-is-largely-up-to-users/

2. *Stripe confirmed OpenRouter acquisition; NYT sources put it at $7.5B*
   Stripe confirmed Wednesday it is buying OpenRouter. NYT sources put the price at $7.5B, up from a $1.3B May valuation, after Stripe outbid Databricks. OpenRouter said product and commitments stay unchanged through a close in a few weeks.
   Why it matters: production traffic on OpenRouter is about to sit on Stripe's ledger; lock terms or dual-home a gateway before close.
   https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/19/stripe-didnt-really-buy-openrouter-because-of-the-singularity/

3. *TrueForge open-sources an MIT agent harness vs Claude Managed Agents*
   TrueFoundry released TrueForge, the production harness it already runs (agent loop, sandbox-as-a-tool, approvals, session persistence). `npx @truefoundry/trueforge` runs it locally; a hosted pay-per-use tier is live. It claims quality parity with Claude Managed Agents at ~30% lower cost on the same model.
   Why it matters: teams that do not want Anthropic as both model and runtime can trial a vendor-neutral harness this week.
   https://www.truefoundry.com/blog/engineering/trueforge-open-source-agent-harness/

4. *White House pushed a "fair" Clarity Act; CFTC innovation panel sits today*
   At a Wednesday White House meeting with Coinbase, Ripple, Robinhood, Kraken, a16z crypto, and the SEC and CFTC chairs, Trump told Congress to pass a "fair version" of the Clarity Act. The bill returns to the Senate in September and still needs about six Democratic votes; Trump also said CFTC Chair Selig is working to bring Hyperliquid into the US under a compliant setup.
   Why it matters: market-structure lobbying now has a public clock before the September cloture test.
   https://decrypt.co/376006/trump-pass-fair-version-clarity-white-house-crypto-meeting
